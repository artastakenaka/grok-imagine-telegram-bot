## What is Grok Imagine Telegram Bot?

**Grok Imagine Telegram Bot** is a Telegram bot for AI image and AI video generation powered by Grok Imagine image and video models.

It brings the creative Grok Imagine workflow into Telegram, so you can generate images, edit uploaded images, and create short videos without opening a separate app or buying a fixed monthly subscription.

Just send a prompt in Telegram and the bot can turn it into an image or video. You can also upload images as references, choose image quality, adjust aspect ratios, and manage your balance with Telegram Stars.

## Available Modes

Grok Imagine Telegram Bot supports several creative modes:

- 🎨 **Standard Image** — fast AI image generation for everyday prompts
- 🖼️ **Pro Image** — higher quality image generation with 1k and 2k resolution options
- 🎬 **Video** — AI video generation from text prompts or uploaded images

## Image Generation Features

- Generate AI images directly in Telegram
- Create images from text prompts
- Use uploaded images as visual references
- Choose image aspect ratio: 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 3:4, 2:3, or Auto
- Choose image resolution: 1k or 2k where available
- Switch between Standard and Pro image modes
- Pay only for the images you generate after the free weekly usage

## Video Generation Features

- Generate short AI videos inside Telegram
- Create videos from text prompts
- Animate uploaded images
- Send up to 7 reference images for video generation
- Choose video aspect ratio: Auto, 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 3:4, 3:2, or 2:3
- Choose duration presets from 1 to 15 seconds
- Choose 480p or 720p video resolution
- Pay only for the seconds and resolution you actually use
